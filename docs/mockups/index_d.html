twiqo · green direction (mockup D)

Two changes to what you have today: green becomes the main colour (buttons, active states, prices, highlights) with blue kept for Move and links, and the layout is reorganised around what you want to do rather than a social feed, so it stops reading as Facebook. Nothing is built yet: this is the proposal to react to.

2 · What changes in the layout
Home is a “what do you want to do?” screen, not a feedGreeting + your location, a big search, four action tiles (Buy, Sell, Send, Drive), and a Your day card with the things that need you (offers, pickups). The social feed moves to its own Community tab.
Three modes instead of a social menuShop (green), Move (blue) and Community. On desktop the modes sit in the top bar as a switch; there is no left menu and no right-hand contacts list, which is the Facebook giveaway.
Floating pill navigationDetached from the screen edges; the active tab expands to show its name; a raised centre “+”. Profile lives behind your avatar in the header, so the bar is Home · Market · + · Trips · Inbox.
Posts become “notes”Colour rail on the left (green for posts, blue for transport, amber for events), a single small reaction row and one Reply button. No wide Like · Comment · Share bar with dividers.
Stories become “Moments”Tall rounded tiles instead of circles with rings, shown below your day, not above everything.
Market and Move lead with the useful bitsCategory tiles instead of chips, a “top pick” hero card, prices as navy badges on photos. Move starts with a route card (From → To) and shows drivers with a capacity bar and trust badges.

DesktopThe mode switch (Shop · Move · Community) replaces the usual nav bar items; there is no left menu and no contacts column. The wide column is for doing things, the narrow one is “Your day”.
What would stay the same
All the featuresNothing is removed. Messages, offers, bookings, circles, clips and settings are all still there, just arranged differently.
The brandLogo, name in lowercase, Plus Jakarta Sans, and the friendly rounded look. The logo's blue tick stays blue.
AccessibilityEvery colour pairing here is chosen to pass WCAG AA text contrast (white on the green is 5.0:1).
